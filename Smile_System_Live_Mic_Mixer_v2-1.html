🎙️ Microphone access

Smile System needs microphone permission to process your live voice through Echo, Low, Mid and High. Audio is processed locally in this browser; it is not uploaded by this page.

Before starting: lower your speaker volume to prevent loud feedback.

Your browser will show its own permission prompt. If you deny it, enable microphone access in browser site settings.
